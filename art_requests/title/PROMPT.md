## logo — 로고
```
Create ONE image: a game title LOGO for a 2D cartoon war game called "BAND OF MICE", where rat soldiers fight cat soldiers in a fictional WW2-style war.
Background: a completely flat solid pure magenta (#FF00FF) background filling the whole canvas, with NO gradient, NO shadow and NO glow on the background (it will be keyed out).
Design: a WW2 army unit shoulder-patch / stencil emblem feel. The words "BAND OF MICE" in bold, slightly worn military stencil capital letters, khaki-cream letters with a dark olive-brown outline, "BAND OF" smaller on top, "MICE" large below. Behind or beside the words, a simple flat emblem: a round olive helmet with two round mouse ears poking out of it, and two crossed rifles. Small banner ribbon under the title, left empty (no text on it).
STYLE: flat matte 2D, large simple shapes, at most two flat shading tones, muted palette (olive drab, khaki, cream, mud brown, a small dark red accent), no gloss, no sparkles, no 3D bevel, no photo texture. Spell the text EXACTLY "BAND OF MICE" and include NO other text or letters anywhere. Wide landscape layout, the logo centered with generous magenta margin on all sides.
```

## title_bg — 메인메뉴 키 아트
```
Create ONE wide 16:9 landscape illustration for the main menu of a 2D cartoon war game "Band of Mice", where rats fight cats in a fictional WW2-style war.
STYLE — MUST MATCH the attached key art (ref_keyart.png) as if painted by the same artist on the same day: flat matte 2D illustration, large simple shapes, VERY LOW detail, soft flat color areas with at most one or two flat shading tones, no line-art outlines (or only very soft dark edges), NO fine texture, NO hatching, NO glossy highlights, NO sparkles, NO anime / Ghibli / storybook look, simple faces with small dot eyes. Desaturated muted palette: olive drab, khaki, mud brown, warm grey, smoke beige, dusty sky with a warm orange haze; depth via flat grey haze layers.
Scene: late afternoon on a muddy WW2 front line. On the LEFT TWO-THIRDS, a squad of six rat soldiers (drawn exactly like the rats in the key art: grey-brown upright rats with big round pink ears, pink nose, olive round helmets and olive uniforms) resting together on a trench parapet of sandbags and broken fence posts: one rat lighting a tiny candle stub, one cleaning a rifle, one with a bandaged arm, one young rat looking toward the horizon, one with a radio pack, an older sergeant rat with a scar standing and looking back at them. Warm, quiet, brotherly mood before a battle. Behind them, a hazy battlefield with barbed wire, a ruined windmill, and far in the distance on the horizon dark silhouettes of cat helmets and a tank.
The RIGHT ONE-THIRD of the image must be calm and empty: just hazy orange sky and soft dark ground fading toward the right edge, with no characters or important details there (a menu will be placed on top).
NO text, NO letters, NO logo anywhere. Cartoon style, no gore.
```
