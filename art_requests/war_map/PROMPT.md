```
Create ONE wide 16:9 illustration (landscape, 1536x864 or larger): a top-down strategic war map of a FICTIONAL continent for a 2D cartoon war game where rats fight cats (WW2 mood).
STYLE — MUST MATCH the attached key art (ref_keyart.png) and HQ background (ref_lobby.jpg) as if painted by the same artist: flat matte 2D illustration, large simple shapes, VERY LOW detail, flat color areas with at most one or two flat shading tones, no outlines, no fine texture, no hatching, no glossy highlights, NOT a detailed fantasy RPG map. Desaturated muted palette: olive drab, khaki, mud brown, warm grey, dusty slate-blue water, smoke beige paper. Few, simple, chunky landmark shapes instead of many tiny ones. It should read as a simple printed military campaign map lying on the rats' command table: plain aged beige paper, faint fold creases, soft vignette at the edges.
The attached ref_layout.png is an OLD version of this same map: copy ONLY where things are (coastlines, the river course and bridge position, forest, factory city, sewer city, trench line, capital) — do NOT copy its detailed rendering style.
Geography from SOUTH (bottom) to NORTH (top):
- Bottom edge: a coastline with a sandy beach on the bottom-left and a small harbor; a rats' underground sewer city on the bottom-center (round manhole covers, tunnel mouths) — the Rat Republic homeland.
- Lower middle: farmland fields and small villages with red roofs, a ruined industrial city with factories and chimneys on the right side.
- Middle: a wide river crossing the map from left to right with ONE prominent stone bridge near the center; a dense snowy pine forest on the left side of the river.
- Upper middle: wide open grassy steppe plains with tank tracks and craters, a long trench line with barbed wire.
- Top: a coast with concrete bunkers on the top-left, and the cats' imperial capital city on the top-center: grey stone, tall towers, a big domed palace, dark red banners with a simple cat-paw emblem.
Small tiny icons only (trees, roofs, chimneys, bunkers), no characters, no arrows, no unit markers, NO hex grid, NO text, NO labels, NO legend, NO compass rose text. Keep the center areas of each region fairly clean and readable so that hex tiles can be drawn on top later.
```
